About Sentinel X

Empowering safety through innovation

Purpose

Sentinel X is designed to provide instant emergency response for individuals who may face sudden health crises, accidents, or dangerous situations. Our wearable device ensures that help is just a button press away.

Problem Statement

Traditional emergency systems often rely on internet connectivity and smartphone access. In critical situations, these may not be available. Elderly individuals, children, and people with medical conditions need a reliable, always-on safety solution.

Solution

Sentinel X uses GSM technology to send SMS alerts without internet, combined with GPS for accurate location tracking. The device automatically detects falls, fire, and temperature anomalies, triggering emergency protocols when needed.

Benefits

  • Works without internet connectivity
  • Automatic emergency detection
  • Real-time guardian monitoring
  • Accurate GPS location sharing
  • Secure cloud data storage
  • User-friendly wearable design
